History of AIGNER

A history of Tradition and Visionary Spirit - The story of Aigner begins with its pioneering founder, the Swiss watch maker Fritz Meyer (1859-1926). It was born from the idea to create a robust yet elegant watch. Fritz Meyer develops their own caliber, the company naming it the "38" after his 38th birthday. In 1905, Fritz Meyer enters into a partnership with Johann Studeli, and creates the legendary MST in-house movement for Aigner.
